Card 01 Divisibility Modulo · William's Focus
💡 Mnemonic: Split 72 into 8 and 9! Last 3 digits determine Y, sum of all digits determines X. Complete both steps!
[Parallel Practice Challenge]
The six-digit number $\overline{73A64B}$ is divisible by both $8$ and $9$. Find $A$ and $B$.

① Last 3 digits: $\overline{64B} \div 8$ has no remainder, so $B = $ ________;
② Sum of digits: $7+3+A+6+4+B$ is a multiple of 9, so $A = $ ________.
[Key]: $B = 0$ or $8$. If $B=0$, $A=7$ (737640); if $B=8$, $A=8$ (738648).
Card 02 Decimal Addition & Division · William
💡 Mnemonic: Ten tenths make one whole! $0.9+0.1 = 1.0$. In division, shift the decimal point equally: $5.4\div 0.6 = 54\div 6 = 9$!
[Mental Speed Drill]
(1) $0.8 + 0.2 = $ ____________
(2) $4.9 + 0.1 = $ ____________
(3) $7.2 \div 0.8 = $ ____________ (⚠️ NOT 0.9!)
(4) $40 \div 0.5 = $ ____________
[Key]: (1) 1.0; (2) 5.0; (3) 9; (4) 80.
Card 03 Powers vs Products · Both Students
💡 Mnemonic: Base multiplies itself! $2^3$ means three 2s multiplied: $2\times 2\times 2 = 8$, NEVER $2\times 3 = 6$!
[Write Out Full Expanded Form]
(1) $2^3 = $ ____________________ $= $ ________
(2) $3^4 = $ ____________________ $= $ ________
(3) $10^3 \times 10^4 = 10^{\underline{\quad} + \underline{\quad}} = 10^{\underline{\quad}}$
[Key]: (1) $2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$; (2) $3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 = 81$; (3) $10^7$.
Card 04 Square Numbers · Sophia's Focus
💡 Mnemonic: Twin identical factors make a square! $2\times 3 = 6$ is a rectangle, so 6 is NEVER a square!
[Identification & Geometry]
(1) Circle the square numbers: $12, 16, 18, 25, 27, 36, 45, 49$
(2) A square has an area of $49\text{ cm}^2$. What is its side length?
Answer: Side length $= $ ________ $\text{cm}$ (Do NOT write $49 \div 2$).
[Key]: (1) Square numbers: 16, 25, 36, 49; (2) Side length $= 7\text{ cm}$ ($7 \times 7 = 49$).
Card 05 Fraction Expansion · Sophia's Focus
💡 Mnemonic: What you do to the bottom, you MUST do to the top! Never leave the numerator unscaled!
[Find and Fix the Error]
Mistake: $\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{2+1}{12} = \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{1}{4}$
Correct solution:
$\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{\underline{\quad}}{12} + \dfrac{\underline{\quad}}{12} = $ _____________
[Key]: Expression $= \dfrac{8}{12} + \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{11}{12}$.
Card 06 Smart Fraction Comparison
💡 Mnemonic: Equal numerators? Smaller denominator is bigger! Close to 1? Smaller residual distance is bigger!
[Fill with $>$ or $<$, state rule]
(1) $\dfrac{9}{16}$ ______ $\dfrac{9}{20}$ (Same numerators)
(2) $\dfrac{19}{20}$ ______ $\dfrac{29}{30}$ (Compare distance to 1)
[Key]: (1) $>$; (2) $<$ ($1-\dfrac{19}{20}=\dfrac{1}{20} > 1-\dfrac{29}{30}=\dfrac{1}{30}$).
