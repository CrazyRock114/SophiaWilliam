💡 Key Rules & Common Mistake Prevention:
① Order of Operations (BODMAS): Brackets $\to$ Orders/Powers $\to$ Division & Multiplication (left to right) $\to$ Addition & Subtraction (left to right). Never add brackets randomly or do addition before multiplication without brackets!
② Square Area vs. Side Length: Area $= \text{Side} \times \text{Side} = s^2$. To find side length from area: $s = \sqrt{\text{Area}}$, NEVER $\text{Area} \div 2$! If Area $= 36\text{ cm}^2$, side $= 6\text{ cm}$ (since $6 \times 6 = 36$), NOT $18\text{ cm}$!
③ Metric Volume & Area Scale Factors: $1\text{ m} = 100\text{ cm} \implies 1\text{ m}^2 = 10,000\text{ cm}^2 \implies 1\text{ m}^3 = 1,000,000\text{ cm}^3$. $1\text{ Liter} = 1,000\text{ mL} = 1,000\text{ cm}^3$.
④ Speed, Distance, Time Units: 40 minutes is $\frac{40}{60} = \frac{2}{3}$ of an hour, NEVER 0.4 hours! When speed is in km/h, time must be in hours!
