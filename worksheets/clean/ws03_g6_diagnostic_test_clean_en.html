Section 3: Smart Comparisons & Application (22 pts)
11. (a) $\dfrac{13}{20} > \dfrac{13}{24}$ (Same numerator, smaller denominator is greater); (b) $\dfrac{19}{21} < \dfrac{23}{25}$ (Residual to 1: $1-\dfrac{19}{21}=\dfrac{2}{21} > 1-\dfrac{23}{25}=\dfrac{2}{25}$); (c) $\dfrac{5}{6} = \dfrac{15}{18} > \dfrac{7}{9} = \dfrac{14}{18}$.
