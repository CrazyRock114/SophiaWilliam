3. [Fraction of a Remaining Quantity]
A water tank has a capacity of 480 liters. On Monday, $\frac{3}{8}$ of the water was used. On Tuesday, $\frac{2}{5}$ of the remaining water was used. How many liters of water are left in the tank?
Step-by-step Solution:
🔑 Complete Answer Key & Pedagogical Notes
Section 1 (Equivalent):
(a) 12 ($3 \times 4$); (b) 56 ($8 \times 7$); (c) 3 ($18 \div 6$);
(d) $\frac{3}{4}$ (divide by 15); (e) $\frac{2}{3}$ (divide by 14); (f) 16 ($4 \times 4$).
Section 2 (Conversions):
(a) $2\frac{4}{24} = 2\frac{1}{6}$ (Student Exam check: do not flip quotient & remainder!);
(b) $6\frac{2}{6} = 6\frac{1}{3}$; (c) $9\frac{2}{5}$;
(d) $\frac{31}{7}$ ($4 \times 7 + 3$); (e) $\frac{47}{9}$; (f) $6\frac{3}{12} = 6\frac{1}{4}$.
Section 3 (Add/Subtract):
(a) $\frac{15}{20} + \frac{8}{20} = \frac{23}{20} = 1\frac{3}{20}$;
(b) $\frac{20}{24} - \frac{9}{24} = \frac{11}{24}$;
(c) $2\frac{5}{15} + 1\frac{9}{15} = 3\frac{14}{15}$;
(d) $2\frac{5}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4} = 1\frac{2}{4} = 1\frac{1}{2}$ (borrow 1 as $\frac{4}{4}$);
(e) $3\frac{9}{7} - 2\frac{5}{7} = 1\frac{4}{7}$;
(f) $\frac{14}{20} - \frac{5}{20} + \frac{8}{20} = \frac{17}{20}$.
Section 4 & 5 (Mult/Div & Applications):
Sec 4: (a) $\frac{1}{6}$; (b) $\frac{20}{3} = 6\frac{2}{3}$; (c) $\frac{7}{10} \times \frac{15}{14} = \frac{3}{4}$; (d) $6 \times \frac{4}{3} = 8$;
(e) $\frac{9}{4} \times \frac{4}{3} \times \frac{10}{9} = \frac{10}{3} = 3\frac{1}{3}$.
Sec 5: 1. (a) $\frac{5}{12} = \frac{20}{48} < \frac{21}{48} = \frac{7}{16}$; (b) $1-\frac{1}{12} < 1-\frac{1}{13} \implies \frac{11}{12} < \frac{12}{13}$; (c) $\frac{7}{9} > \frac{7}{11}$.
2. (a) $\frac{3}{5} + 1\frac{1}{2} = \frac{6}{10} + 1\frac{5}{10} = 2\frac{1}{10}$ kg; (b) No; $2\frac{1}{4} - 2\frac{1}{10} = 2\frac{5}{20} - 2\frac{2}{20} = \frac{3}{20}$ kg short.
3. Remaining after Mon: $480 \times (1 - \frac{3}{8}) = 300$ L. Tue used: $300 \times \frac{2}{5} = 120$ L. Left: $300 - 120 = 180$ L.
