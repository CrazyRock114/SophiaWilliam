💡 Key Conceptual Anchors:
① Powers vs. Multiplication: $a^b$ means multiplying $a$ by itself $b$ times! $2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$. It is NEVER $2 \times 3 = 6$!
② 16 has Four 2s Multiplied, NOT 8 Twos: $16 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 2^4$. $2 + 2 + \dots + 2 = 16$ is addition, not factorization!
③ Multiplying Powers: $a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$. When multiplying powers with the same base, you ADD exponents: $10^4 \times 10^5 = 10^9$ (NEVER $10^{20}$).
④ Power of Zero: Any non-zero number to power 0 equals 1: $10^0 = 1$ (NOT $10$).
