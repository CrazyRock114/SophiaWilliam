5. [Bilingual Word Problem · Speed and Distance]
Leo cycles at an average speed of $15\text{ km/h}$. He rides for $40\text{ minutes}$. How far does he ride?
Step 1 Identify units and convert:
Speed is in $\text{km/h}$, but time is given as $40\text{ minutes}$. You MUST convert minutes to hours!
Time $t = 40\text{ minutes} = \dfrac{40}{60}\text{ hours} = \dfrac{\underline{\quad}}{\underline{\quad}}\text{ hours}$.
Step 2 Apply the distance formula:
Formula: $\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$
$S = 15 \times \dfrac{\underline{\quad}}{\underline{\quad}} = $ ____________________ $\text{km}$.
Step 3 Conclude with units:
Answer: Leo rides ____________________ $\text{km}$.
