Section 3: Fractions Operations
1. (1) $3\frac{5}{6}$; (2) $\frac{17}{7}$; (3) $1\frac{1}{3}$; (4) $\frac{7}{12}$.
2. ① $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{5} = \frac{10}{15} + \frac{3}{15} = \frac{13}{15}$;
② $2\frac{1}{3} + 3\frac{5}{6} = 2\frac{2}{6} + 3\frac{5}{6} = 5\frac{7}{6} = 6\frac{1}{6}$;
③ $4\frac{1}{4} - 1\frac{2}{3} = 4\frac{3}{12} - 1\frac{8}{12} = 3\frac{15}{12} - 1\frac{8}{12} = 2\frac{7}{12}$;
④ $\frac{5}{8} - \frac{1}{6} + \frac{7}{12} = \frac{15}{24} - \frac{4}{24} + \frac{14}{24} = \frac{25}{24} = 1\frac{1}{24}$.
3. ① $\frac{5}{12} \div \frac{15}{16} = \frac{5}{12} \times \frac{16}{15} = \frac{4}{9}$;
② $\frac{7}{18} \times \frac{9}{14} \div \frac{5}{6} = \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{6}{5} = \frac{3}{10}$;
③ $\left(\frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{6}\right) \times 12 = \frac{3}{4} \times 12 + \frac{1}{6} \times 12 = 9 + 2 = 11$;
④ $\frac{5}{9} \div \left(\frac{8}{12} - \frac{3}{12}\right) = \frac{5}{9} \div \frac{5}{12} = \frac{5}{9} \times \frac{12}{5} = \frac{4}{3} = 1\frac{1}{3}$.
